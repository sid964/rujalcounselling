About The Counsellor

FAQ

Before your first session.

What happens during the first session?

+

The first session is a gentle conversation to understand your concerns, comfort level, background, and what kind of support may help you.

Is counselling confidential?

+

Counselling is handled with privacy, care, and professional boundaries. Confidentiality can be discussed clearly before sessions begin.

How many sessions will I need?

+

It depends on your goals and concerns. Some people need a few focused sessions, while others prefer ongoing support at a comfortable pace.

How long does each session last?

+

Session length can be confirmed while booking. Most counselling sessions are planned to allow enough time for a calm and meaningful conversation.

Who can benefit from counselling?

+

Anyone feeling stressed, stuck, emotionally overwhelmed, confused, low, or ready for personal growth can benefit from counselling support.

Do I need a diagnosis before seeking counselling?

+

No. You can seek counselling even if you do not have a diagnosis. It is enough to feel that you need support, clarity, or a safe place to talk.

What issues can counselling help with?

+

Counselling can support anxiety, stress, low mood, grief, relationship concerns, anger, communication, self-esteem, and emotional well-being.

Can I stop therapy whenever I want?

+

Yes. You remain in control of your counselling journey. You can discuss pauses, endings, or changes in frequency whenever needed.

Do I need to know exactly what to say?

+

No. You can begin with whatever feels easiest. The conversation will be guided gently and at your pace.
