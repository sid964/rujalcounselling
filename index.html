Anxiety is more than normal worry.

Anxiety can feel like constant overthinking, fear, restlessness, panic, tightness in the body, or expecting something to go wrong.

In counselling, we may explore:

What triggers the worry

How the body reacts to stress

Before you begin.

Is counselling confidential?

+

Sessions are handled with privacy and care. You can ask about confidentiality before starting.

Do I need to know what to say?

+

No. You can begin with whatever feels easiest. The conversation will be guided gently.

Is this a crisis service?

+

No. If you feel at immediate risk, contact local emergency services or a trusted person near you.
