Anxiety

Anxiety is more than ordinary worry.

It can feel like racing thoughts, a tight body, panic, restlessness, or always expecting something to go wrong.

In counselling, we gently look at:

What triggers the worry, how your body responds, and what helps you feel steady again.

+

+

Is this a crisis service?

+

No. If you feel at immediate risk, contact local emergency services or a trusted person near you.
