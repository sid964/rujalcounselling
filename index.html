A Gentle Reminder

You do not have to wait until things become unbearable.

Counselling is not only for crisis. It can help when you feel stuck, tired, confused, disconnected or unsure how to move forward.

A supportive conversation can bring relief, perspective and a clearer next step.

Questions

Before your first session.

Is counselling confidential?

+

Yes, counselling is handled with privacy and care. You can discuss confidentiality before beginning.

What happens in the first session?

+

The first session is usually about understanding your concerns, your background and what kind of support you need.

Do I need to know exactly what to say?

+

No. You can begin with whatever feels easiest. The counsellor will help guide the conversation gently.
