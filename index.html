FAQ

Before your first session.

Is counselling confidential?

+

Counselling is handled with privacy and care. You can discuss confidentiality clearly before beginning your sessions.

What happens in the first session?

+

The first session is usually about understanding your concerns, your background and what kind of support may help.

Do I need to know exactly what to say?

+

No. You can start with whatever feels easiest. The conversation will be guided gently.
