Anxiety

Anxiety can feel like your mind is always preparing for danger.

It may show up as overthinking, fear, panic, restlessness, body tension, difficulty sleeping, or feeling unable to relax.

In counselling, we gently look at:

what triggers the worry, how your body responds, and what helps you feel steady again.
