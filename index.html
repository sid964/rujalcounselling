What We Work With

When feelings become difficult to carry.

Anxiety

Anxiety can feel like your mind is always preparing for danger.

It may show up as overthinking, restlessness, panic, body tightness, or fear that something will go wrong.

In sessions, we gently understand:

what triggers the worry, how your body responds, and what helps you feel steady again.
