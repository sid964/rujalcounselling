FAQ

Before your first session.

Is counselling confidential?

+

Counselling is handled with privacy, care, and professional boundaries. You can discuss confidentiality clearly before beginning your sessions.

What happens in the first session?

+

The first session is mainly about understanding your concerns, comfort level, background, and what kind of support may help you.

Do I need to know exactly what to say?

+

No. You can begin with whatever feels easiest. The conversation will be guided at your pace.
